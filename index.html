ABOUT SPACEECHO

Remembering the machines
that moved us forward.

SpaceEcho is an independent educational storytelling project created for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge. It is designed for curious school-age explorers and anyone who wants to understand the human stories behind space hardware.

Evidence matters. We distinguish confirmed facts from illustrative visuals and avoid assigning precise locations when mission records do not establish them. The full-globe Moon and Mars overview illustrations are AI-generated and are not NASA imagery or scientific basemaps. Mission photographs are shown separately with captions and source credits. Story pins are illustrative and are not precise geographic coordinates.

SpaceEcho is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official product of NASA. NASA names and public data are referenced for educational purposes. Mission images and data remain subject to their original source terms.
