# SpaceEcho — Image credits and visual provenance

SpaceEcho contains several different kinds of visuals. **NASA photographs are identified by their NASA image IDs and source pages. NASA artist concepts and models are not photographs. AI-generated illustrations are explicitly identified as AI-generated and are not presented as NASA imagery.** SpaceEcho is an independent educational project and is not endorsed by NASA.

## NASA mission photographs and NASA archival images included locally

- **Spirit landing-site panorama, Sol 16, Gusev Crater (PIA05117)** — `images/spirit-landing-site-pia05117.webp`. NASA/JPL-Caltech/Cornell. NASA Photojournal record: https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/catalog/PIA05117. The panorama shows the landing platform and airbags; it is not a close-up photograph of the Spirit rover itself.
- **Opportunity “Lion King” panorama (PIA05755)** — `images/opportunity-lion-king-pia05755.webp`. NASA/JPL/Cornell. NASA source: https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/opportunity-captures-lion-king-panorama/.
- **Mars Pathfinder lander photographed by Sojourner (PIA04315)** — `images/pathfinder-sojourner-second-rover-image-pia04315.jpg`. NASA/JPL/Caltech. NASA source: https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/the-second-rover-image/.
- **InSight SEIS deployed on Mars (PIA22956)** — `images/insight-seis-deployed-pia22956.jpg`. NASA/JPL-Caltech. NASA source: https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/seis-deployed-on-mars/.
- **InSight SEIS deployment animation (PIA22956)** — `images/insight-seis-deployment-pia22956.gif`. NASA/JPL-Caltech. NASA source: https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/seis-deployed-on-mars/.
- **Ingenuity deployed on Mars (PIA24541)** — `images/ingenuity-deployed-pia24541.jpg`. NASA/JPL-Caltech. The image was taken by Perseverance’s rear Hazard Camera on April 4, 2021. NASA source: https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/ingenuity-deployed-on-mars/.
- **Apollo 12 astronaut Pete Conrad at Surveyor 3 (AS12-48-7134)** — `images/apollo12-surveyor3-as12-48-7134.jpg`. NASA / Apollo 12, photograph by Alan Bean. NASA APOD context: https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap241115.html. Original frame is part of the Apollo 12 image sequence; Apollo Journal frame reference: https://www.apollojournals.org/alsj/a12/images12.html.
- **Apollo 14 ALSEP deployment (AS14-67-9361)** — `images/apollo14-alsep-as14-67-9361.jpg`. NASA/JSC. The image documents ALSEP deployment during EVA 1. NASA identifier and description: https://www.dvidshub.net/image/857870/apollo-14-mission-image-view-alsep-station. Apollo 14 frame index: https://www.apollojournals.org/alsj/a14/images14.html.
- **Phoenix lander seen from orbit (PIA10701)** — `images/phoenix-orbital-pia10701.jpg`. NASA/JPL-Caltech/University of Arizona. This is an enhanced-color HiRISE image showing the lander on Mars. NASA source: https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/color-image-of-phoenix-lander-on-mars-surface/.
- **Phoenix landing-site panorama (PIA10735)** — `images/phoenix-site-panorama-pia10735.jpg`. NASA/JPL-Caltech/University of Arizona/Texas A&M. NASA source: https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/phoenix-site-panorama/.
- **Pioneer 11 Saturn, rings and Titan flyby image** — `images/pioneer11-saturn-rings.jpeg`. NASA/Ames. This is a genuine image taken by Pioneer 11 during its September 1, 1979 Saturn flyby, not a generic Saturn illustration. NASA source: https://science.nasa.gov/image-detail/amf-cf2ae57d-b5f0-42ea-b5e3-c955208bc278/.
- **Pioneer plaque mounted on spacecraft structure (ARC-1972-A72-2137)** — `images/pioneer-plaque-spacecraft-arc.jpg`. NASA Ames Research Center archival photograph. The plaque design was carried by Pioneer 10 and Pioneer 11. NASA plaque background: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/pioneer-plaque/.
- **Pioneer plaque close-up** — `images/pioneer-plaque-closeup.png`. NASA/Ames plaque image. Both Pioneer 10 and Pioneer 11 carried the plaque design. NASA source: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/pioneer-plaque/.

## Attribution and presentation rules

1. Keep the individual NASA image ID and credit with each NASA photograph.
2. Do not call NASA artist concepts, models, or infographics photographs.
3. Keep the phrase **AI-generated illustration** visible wherever an AI-generated image appears, including fallback states.
4. Do not imply that NASA created or endorsed the AI-generated illustrations.
5. The Moon and Mars globe backgrounds are not scientific basemaps; their story pins are illustrative.
6. NASA mission pages are linked as factual mission references; a mission page is not automatically the source of every image displayed in that mission panel.
